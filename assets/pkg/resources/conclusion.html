Next steps

  1. Open fido2kpxc from Applications. A key icon appears in the menu bar. If macOS refuses to open the app, click Done, then click "Open Anyway" in System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  2. Choose "Set Up…" in the menu. Type a folder for the vault or pick one with "Choose…", then save.
  3. Enter the database password twice and the key's PIN, then touch the key twice.
  4. In the last panel, choose "Grant Accessibility…" and allow fido2kpxc in System Settings. The panel also offers "Start at Login".

The README at github.com/BJMCox/fido2kpxc covers backup keys, several databases, and the terminal commands.
